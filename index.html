Our Story

Trusted Health Intelligence Since 2009

Health Census was founded by a multidisciplinary team of endocrinologists, cardiologists, and data scientists who believed that accurate, accessible health information could save lives. What began as a small patient resource portal has grown into one of the most trusted clinical health intelligence platforms in the region.

We partner with leading hospitals and research institutions to keep every piece of information current, accurate, and actionable — helping patients and caregivers make informed decisions every single day.
